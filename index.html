It is a busy afternoon at a university club fair. You’re beside the debate society table. A new student is standing alone, holding a campus map.

Local language, real choice

At a club fair in Bangladesh, students may greet each other in Bangla first, then switch to English for practice. Choose language to include someone—not to test where they are from.

Make room for everyoneUse the names and pronouns a person gives you. Invite both people to speak; don’t assume they share the same interests.
