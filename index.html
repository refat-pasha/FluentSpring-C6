LESSON 01 · COMMUNICATION

Introducing yourself and others 👋

Think of every introduction as a flight: take off (greet), cruise (connect), bring a passenger aboard (introduce), and land safely (close warmly).

Beginner

Today's flight plan

1. Take off — open the conversation ✈️

Start with a greeting and your name, like a plane lifting off the runway.

“Hi, I'm Tanvir. Nice to meet you!”
2. Cruise — connect at cruising altitude ☁️

Share one detail and ask a question, keeping the conversation flying smoothly.

“I'm studying engineering. What about you?”
3. Board a passenger — introduce another person 🛫

Say both people's names and explain how you know them, like helping someone find their seat.

“This is my friend Sadia. Sadia, meet Tanvir.”
4. Land safely — invite and close politely 🛬

Make the other person feel included before you say goodbye.

“Would you like to join us?” · “It was lovely meeting you!”

Teacher's corner 💡

Remember: A good introduction is a two-way exchange. Share a little about yourself, then give the other person a chance to speak.

The flight analogy

Greeting = take-off (the exciting start). Sharing details = cruising (keeping it smooth). Introducing others = boarding (helping someone aboard). Closing politely = landing (bringing everyone down safely). If you skip the landing, the conversation crashes!

Real life: where does this happen?

✈️ At the airport check-in desk · 🎓 Meeting a new classmate · 💼 A job interview · 🏏 Joining a new cricket team · 🎉 A family wedding. Every place needs the same four moves.

Useful language

“I'm interested in…” · “Have you met…?” · “What about you?” · “See you around!”

Common mistake

❌ “I am study English.”

✅ “I am studying English.”

Use am/is/are + verb-ing for an action happening now.
